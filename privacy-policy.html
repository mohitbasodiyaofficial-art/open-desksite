03 / PRIVACY POLICY

Plain language. Fewer surprises.

Last updated August 29, 2026. This page explains what happens when you contact the Open Desk.

PRIVATE
BY DEFAULT
READING NOTE

We keep this short because privacy information should be understandable.

What we collect

When you submit a support or feedback form, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, topic, and message. We may also receive basic technical information needed to protect the form from abuse.

Why we use it

We use your information to read, respond to, and improve support requests and product feedback. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep messages only as long as reasonably needed to respond, maintain support records, and understand recurring issues. You can ask us to delete your message by emailing privacy@opendesk.help.

Third-party form delivery

This website uses a form-delivery provider to transmit submissions to our support inbox. The provider processes submitted data to deliver the message and may apply its own privacy and security terms. Review the provider’s current terms before publishing if your actual setup differs.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@opendesk.help.
